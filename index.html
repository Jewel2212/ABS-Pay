Simple Payment Flow

Create a USDC payment request on Arc, share the link, and let the buyer pay directly from a compatible wallet.

1

Create Payment

Enter your product name, USDC price and seller wallet.

2

Add Media

Select multiple images or add a product video from your phone gallery.

3

Generate Link

ABS Pay uploads the media to Supabase Storage and creates a shareable URL.

4

Share

Send the payment link through your website, social media or messaging app.

5

Buyer Pays

Buyer connects a compatible wallet, switches to Arc and signs the USDC transfer.

6

Seller Receives

USDC is transferred directly to the specified seller wallet.

Direct Blockchain Payment

Buyer transactions are signed by the buyer's wallet and sent directly to the seller wallet on Arc. ABS Pay does not hold customer funds.
